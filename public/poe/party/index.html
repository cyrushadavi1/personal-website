How to play

Cheat card

  • Glue to the carry (5 m). Roll all 4 warcry keys every ~4.5 s, Enduring Cry first - with Al Dhih + Redblade your cooldowns are 2.7 s and buffs last 4.7 s, so everything is permanent.
  • Tap the life flask every ~5 s in combat: it heals ALLIES within 7.5 m, never you. You live on regen (Vitality + Bloodgrip + consecrated ground).
  • Sorrow of the Divine before bosses/spikes (consecrated ground, 6%/s party regen); Granite when swarmed. Quartz auto-fires. Coruscating Elixir stays OUT of the belt until the ES switch - it auto-uses and drops you to 1 life.
  • You never attack, never curse, never wander to loot mid-fight. Your warcries taunt everything onto YOU - that feeds Al Dhih explosions and Flagellant charges by design.
  • Carry buttons: Ancestral (+30% res, +3% max res) and Battlemage's (+2.5% base crit). Rallying Cry and Intimidate are attack-only - nothing for Spark; they exist for the AG and the Champion.
Keybinds
  • Q = Enduring Cry - the most-pressed button in the build (party regen + endurance charges); give it your strongest finger.
  • W = Ancestral Cry, E = Battlemage's Cry, R = Intimidating Cry. Today that is the whole warcry keyboard; at midgame add T = Seismic Cry and Middle Mouse = Rallying Cry (Rallying is deliberately on the worst bind - it is the most skippable).
  • Right Mouse = Flame Dash (your only movement skill after the bow swap; Blink Arrow dies with the bow).
  • Left click = MOVE ONLY. Set it in options today. You have no attack links, and the party rule is that nobody but the carry may apply anything on hit.
  • Flasks: 1 = life flask (the healing engine - manual, rhythmic), 2 = Sorrow of the Divine (manual, situational), 3 = Granite (manual, defensive). Slots 4-5 hold the instilled auto-flasks (Quartz now, Coruscating after the ES switch) so your manual fingers never visit them.
  • Auras (Purity of Elements + Vitality, later Clarity/Flesh and Stone/AG) are cast once per session - bind them temporarily, cast, then rebind the slot to a warcry.
Map loop
  1. At map start: cast Purity of Elements + Vitality (once), then do a full 4-cry sweep on the group so charges/buffs are rolling before the first pack. Each shout takes ~0.39 s (0.8 s base, +38% gem +69% Urgent Orders warcry speed); a full sweep is ~1.6 s.
  2. Move WITH the carry - inside 5 m (Perquil's Toe lucky radius 50 units is your tightest leash; warcry buffs reach 6 m, Druggery healing 7.5 m). If you can't see the carry on screen, you are giving the party nothing.
  3. As the carry approaches each pack, pre-cry: Enduring (Q) first - 10% of max life/s regen (12.5% with Al Dhih's +25% buff effect) + fills everyone's endurance charges instantly (Redblade's infinite Power maxes every per-5-power line) - then W/E/R in any order. Order genuinely does not matter beyond Enduring-first; cooldowns (2.67 s) are shorter than buffs (4.73 s), so it's 'roll your face on 4 keys every ~4.5 s'.
  4. Tap flask key 1 (life flask) whenever it has ≥17 charges and combat is on - roughly every 5 s. That streams ~390 life/s (today, post-Lab-3) to every ally in 7.5 m for ~4 s; ~780/s once Bloodgrip goes on at 74. You cannot press it at pixel-full life (PoE1 rule) - in combat you almost never are; if it refuses, you're full, shrug and re-tap after any chip.
  5. Your taunts pull the pack onto you and past the carry's Spark. Stand your ground briefly - hits feed Flagellant charges (3/hit on Granite+Quartz) and taunted deaths explode via Al Dhih (8% of max life as chaos), which chain-clears trash for the casual players.
  6. Between packs: nothing is on cooldown-starvation, so just run. Re-sweep cries as the next pack comes on screen rather than mid-transit - buffs last 4.7 s and you want them covering the engagement, not the hallway.
  7. Mana check: each cry costs ~49 mana (19 base × 200% Urgent Orders × 130% More Duration). If you go dry at a 4-cry-per-4.5 s pace, pull the midgame plan's Clarity gem forward (1c, spare socket in the 6S chest) rather than dropping supports.
Boss loop
  • Enduring Cry stays on strict cooldown (~2.7 s today) - you are the party's ONLY endurance charge engine, and its 10-12.5%/s regen buff is the party's healing floor through degens. Charges last 10 s - a lapse zeroes everyone's charges and the regen buff, and strict-cooldown recasting makes a lapse impossible, keeping everyone at 3/3 (12% phys reduction + 12% all res).
  • Ancestral Cry is your biggest boss button: +30% all ele res AND +3% maximum ele res to everyone in 6 m. At 75→78 max res that's ~12% less elemental damage taken for the whole party - refresh it before every slam/elemental phase, never let it lapse.
  • Battlemage's Cry on cooldown for DPS: +2.5% BASE crit chance to the Spark carry (a large relative bump on a ~6-7% base crit spell).
  • Intimidating Cry when the fight demands movement (+18% MS to everyone for dodging phases). Its Intimidate line is attack-damage-only - zero for Spark - so it is purely the 'go fast' button.
  • Seismic Cry (midgame+) before physical burst: 25% MORE armour and +75% stun threshold party-wide. Rallying Cry at bosses: skip it unless the AG or the Champion is actually hitting the boss - it adds attack damage only.
  • Drop Sorrow of the Divine at the arena start and re-press as it fades: consecrated ground = 6% max life/s regen for every ally standing in it, and (if the interaction holds - see mistakes) the flask stream also tops the LL carry's ES during its effect. Keep the life flask streaming on cooldown throughout.
  • Position ON the carry, not on the boss. Adds get taunted onto you - peel them off the aurabot and the casual, and let them die in Spark. Respect one-shot telegraphs: you are the squishiest serious member until the ES gear at 84.
Positioning
  • Hard numbers (all verified on poedb): warcry buff/taunt/power radius 60 units = 6 m (grows to ~7.1 m at endgame via Deep Breaths' 40% increased warcry AoE); The Druggery ally-heal radius 75 units = 7.5 m; Perquil's Toe 'Nearby Allies' Damage with Hits is Lucky' radius 50 units = 5 m. Leer Cast's +50% ally damage circle is ~75 units / 7.5 m (poedb version history) - Perquil's 5 m leash is still the binding constraint.
  • Rule of thumb: the 5 m Perquil's leash is your binding constraint - stay within about half a screen of the carry at all times, ideally on top of them.
  • Who must be near whom: the CARRY is the priority body inside all four circles (buffs, heal, lucky, Leer). The aurabot naturally stacks with the carry, so gluing to the carry covers everyone except the casual - call them in for boss starts, don't chase them in maps.
  • Stand ON or slightly behind the carry when crying: taunted mobs converge on you, walking through the carry's Spark on the way - that is free peel plus Flagellant charge income for you.
  • The Animate Guardian (midgame+) hugs you automatically (Meat Shield); it wears Leer Cast at 84+, so its position becomes the +50% damage circle - another reason the party fights in one tight ball.
  • You are never the one who scouts, backtracks, or loots mid-combat. Every metre away from the carry switches off four buffs at once.
What you give the party

Upkeep: recast every ~4.5 s; Redblade's infinite Power maxes it with zero enemies nearby.
  • Ancestral Cry buff (poedb): +5% all ele res per 5 Power (cap 30) → +30% all res, and +1% MAXIMUM ele res per 10 Power (cap 30) → +3% max res, to user and allies. The party's strongest defensive layer; keep permanently rolling.
  • Battlemage's Cry buff (poedb, 3.25 rework): +0.5% Critical Strike Chance per 5 Power (cap 25) → +2.5% BASE crit to user and allies - a direct damage buff to the crit Spark carry.
  • Intimidating Cry buff (poedb): +3% Movement Speed per 5 Power (cap 30) → +18% MS to user and allies. (Its Intimidate = 10% increased ATTACK damage taken - irrelevant to the caster carry.)
  • Seismic Cry buff (midgame+, poedb): 5% MORE armour per 5 Power (cap 25) → 25% more armour, and +15% Stun Threshold per 5 Power (cap 25) → +75%, to user and allies.
  • Rallying Cry (midgame+, poedb): allies gain added ATTACK damage = 4% of your main-hand weapon per 5 Power (cap 30) → 24% of Rebuke of the Vaal's huge 5-type flat, 48% for minions (100% more effect) - feeds ONLY the Animate Guardian and the Champion banner-mate, NOT the Spark carry.
  • The Druggery heal stream (poedb, radius 75): 100% of your life flask recovery applied to allies within 7.5 m. With the q30 Cautious Divine Life Flask of Perenniality: ~390 life/s today post-Lab-3, ~780/s at 74 with Bloodgrip (its +100% increased exactly cancels Master Surgeon's 50% less), ~1,100/s midgame (11× 8% tattoos +88%, Mender's Wellspring +25%, Watcher's Eye +50-70%, Alchemist's Genius +10% effect), ~2,500-5,000/s endgame when Coruscating keeps you at 1 life and the Cautious prefix's 130% MORE is always live. Upkeep: press flask ~every 5 s; charges come from time (Nature's Adrenaline 3/3 s + flask mastery 1/3 s + Druggery belt 1/3 s + Mender's 1/3 s), not kills.
  • Sorrow of the Divine (poedb): consecrated ground on use = 6% max life/s regen to every ally standing in it (verified); during its effect flask life recovery 'also applies to Energy Shield' - the intended ES top-up for the low-life carry (see mistakes for the honesty flag on this line).
  • Leer Cast (poedb, current): 'You and nearby allies gain 50% increased Damage' (you take the 25% reduced-damage line - irrelevant, you deal none). Worn by you until 84, then moved onto the Animate Guardian so the party keeps it.
  • Perquil's Toe (poedb): 'Nearby Allies' Damage with Hits is Lucky', radius 50 - a large real-DPS multiplier for the carry's Spark. Passive; just stay close. (Its downside: enemy LIGHTNING damage vs YOU is lucky - you tank it, not the party.)
  • Auras: Purity of Elements (elemental resistances + elemental ailment immunity to nearby allies - backstop for anyone who drifts from the aurabot) and Vitality (~200+ life/s flat regen aura). Cast once, always on.
  • Utility: every warcry taunts (60-unit radius, +20% taunt duration from Redblade) = constant peel off the squishies; Al Dhih makes taunted enemies explode on death for 8% of max life as chaos - real trash-clear the party will notice.

Common mistakes
  • Equipping Coruscating Elixir at level 70 because 'it's in the build'. It is INSTILLED (auto-uses at full charges) and 'removes all but one Life on use' - while you are life-based with ~no ES it will repeatedly drop you to 1 HP and you will die to breeze. Fix: buy it now (2c), bench it until you have the ES chest/helm (~84) and Sorrow running; only then does 1-life-behind-ES become your defence.
  • Expecting Rallying Cry to buff the carry. Verified current text: allies gain added ATTACK damage from your weapon - Spark is a spell; the carry gets literally zero. Fix: treat Rallying as an AG/Champion-only button on the worst keybind, pressed last, skipped freely. Same logic kills Intimidate (Intimidating Cry + Haunting Shout): 10% increased ATTACK damage taken does nothing for Spark.
  • Trusting the Sorrow-of-the-Divine ES top-up blindly. 'Life Recovery from Flasks also applies to ES during Effect' + Druggery's redirect is a community-DISPUTED interaction - some reports say the ES application may not ride along to allies (or to you) once Druggery redirects the recovery. Fix: test on day one - pop Sorrow, spam the life flask, watch the carry's ES orb. If it doesn't move, Sorrow is still worth pressing for consecrated ground (6%/s, verified) and your own endgame Zealot's Oath loop, but stop advertising ES heals to the party.
  • Skipping Lab 3 'until later'. Without Master Surgeon your own regen cancels the ally heal stream almost instantly (life flask effects end when your unreserved life fills). Fix: run Merciless Lab the day you convert - it is level-68 content. Accept the visible number drop from its 50% less rider; Bloodgrip at 74 cancels it exactly ((1+100% increased) × 0.5 = 1.0) and everything after is profit.
  • Pressing Sorrow constantly while life-based. Its Zealot's Oath line redirects YOUR life regen to a near-zero ES pool for the duration - you self-nerf your only self-heal. Fix: pre-84, Sorrow is a boss/spike button for the party's consecrated ground, not a piano staple.
  • Playing it like an aurabot - standing back. Your buffs are 4.7-6.5 s leashes on a 5-7.5 m radius. Fix: you are a melee-range support; the carry's feet are your position. Related: don't detour for loot during combat - every warcry buff on the party expires ~5 s after you leave.
  • Letting Enduring Cry lapse at bosses because 'buffs are up'. The charges and the 10%/s regen both expire; you are the party's only charge engine. Fix: Enduring is on strict cooldown, always, everywhere - it is the one button with a priority rule.
  • Attacking, cursing, or equipping clever on-hit gear. Party curse limit is 1 and it belongs to the carry - any curse you apply evicts theirs (newest wins). The 3.29 corrupted curse-on-hit glove implicits are the trap to watch when shopping. Fix: left-click = move only; your weapon is a stat stick for Rallying/warcries, never swung.
  • Buying q20 More Duration (39c) or a 6-linked chest. The build's largest link is a 4L (body needs 6 SOCKETS, not links) - the 12-div 6L is the biggest money trap; buy the ~50c 6-socket Twilight Regalia. On quality: q20 More Duration is actually a real ~10% MORE duration - worth grabbing by the Rebuke swap, just not at day-5 prices.

How to play

Cheat card

  • Stay next to the Spark carry. Your body IS the buff - Fortification share and banners only work on people near you.
  • At every fight-stop, press Q (Defiance Banner). If the fight lasts, press W (Dread) about 3 seconds later, then E (War). Never plant while running - a banner dies if you walk away from it.
  • NEVER attack in party (left-click is set to move-only). Your axe curses on hit and would erase the carry's curse.
  • Life flask when hurt. Everything else - warcries, Molten Shell, Valour - is automatic.
  • Boss: Q ... W ... E (3s apart), then R (Embrace Madness, level 70+) on at the start, off when it dies.
Keybinds
  • Left mouse = Move only (Options > left-click bind). This is the single most important bind: once the 500%-CDR axe is equipped, ANY attack applies Vulnerability on hit and evicts the carry's curse (curse limit 1). Move-only makes the mistake impossible.
  • Q = Defiance Banner, W = Dread Banner, E = War Banner. Key order = priority order, so muscle memory always plants the best one first (Defiance: movement speed + spell suppression; Dread: block + life-on-block; War: accuracy/melee phys - least valuable to a spell carry, press last or skip).
  • Right mouse (or Space) = Flame Dash. Your only movement skill; dash freely - the Replica Badge's '20-25% chance to lose a Frenzy Charge on Travel Skill' costs you nothing you use.
  • 1 = Life flask, 2 = Mana flask (keep until Enlighten 3 arrives; Autoexertion reserves most of your 668 mana - only ~126 is free today). 3-5 = utility flasks: put 'reused/auto-use' instilling enchants on them so you never press them; stop investing in them entirely - Toad's Price will disable utility flasks later.
  • R = Embrace Madness (appears at level 70 with Beacon of Madness). A toggle, not a spam button.
  • T (or an out-of-the-way key) = Sunder, SOLO PLAY ONLY. Remove it from the bar when partying once the curse-line axe is equipped. Nothing on this build is ever linked to General's Cry - its Mirage Warriors would swing your cursing axe.
  • Warcries get NO keybind at all: Autoexertion triggers all of them automatically when their cooldowns end ('Supported Warcries will Trigger when their Cooldown is over' - poedb). You cannot and should not press them. (Endgame exception: the optional glove Enduring Cry sits OUTSIDE the 6L and needs its own key; Petrified Blood on those gloves also needs one activation after each login.)
Map loop
  1. Walk with the party, glued to the carry. Nothing to press: your four warcries auto-fire (each ~1.3s cooldown with the CDR axe, General's Cry every ~0.5s), each grants 5 Valour via the tree mastery, so your Valour bar refills 0-to-50 in ~2-3 seconds on its own (today, pre-axe: ~10-11 seconds).
  2. When the party STOPS to fight (rare, essence, ritual, breach, legion, shrine): press Q. Defiance Banner plants at your feet, eating all your Valour - at 50 Valour it is roughly 6x the listed power and roughly a 6m bubble (base 2.4m). Plant it where the carry is standing.
  3. If the fight is still going ~3 seconds later (Valour refilled): press W for Dread. Another ~3s and still fighting: E for War. Spacing matters because each plant consumes ALL Valour - pressing two keys together makes the second banner a dud.
  4. When the party moves on, just leave. The banner dies when you exit its bubble, and that is fine: Inspirational refunds 30% of its Valour and the warcries refill the rest before the next pack. Do not try to 'save' banners.
  5. Press the life flask when your health dips. Molten Shell is automatic (CwDT). Flame Dash to keep up with the carry.
  6. That is the whole map: Flame Dash + 1-3 banner presses per stop + life flask. Planted banners last ~28-34s (10s base, +100% Worthy Causes, +40% More Duration L21, +quality) but in normal mapping you abandon them long before they expire - expected and correct.
Boss loop
  • Arrive with a full 50 Valour automatically (warcries kept it topped while you walked).
  • Plant in the spot where the CARRY will actually stand, not at the arena door: Q (Defiance - party gets big movement speed, spell suppression chance and flat damage reduction from suppressed hits), wait ~3s, W (Dread - party block chance + life recovered on block), wait ~3s, E (War - only helps melee/attack allies; skip it if you are busy staying alive).
  • Level 70+: toggle R (Embrace Madness) ON at the start. Glorious Madness gives +15 maximum Fortification (Armour/Evasion Beacon variant) - shared to the whole party via Fortitude, taking the stack from ~36 to ~51. Downside: you gain a random Touched debuff every 2 seconds while it runs - if you feel wobbly, toggle it OFF (8s lockout before you can re-toggle).
  • During the fight: stand inside your banners next to the carry and do nothing except life flask and the occasional Flame Dash out of ground effects. Warcries keep auto-taunting the boss adds onto you (the tankiest member) and Seismic keeps knocking back/interrupting trash.
  • A banner expires after ~30s or dies when a phase forces you out of it - just replant: 30% Valour refund + ~2-3s refill means a fresh 50-Valour banner is always seconds away. Re-pressing an ACTIVE banner's key replaces it, so only re-press after it is gone or after you have moved.
  • Endgame extra (optional): manual Enduring Cry from the gloves as a panic button - a 0.8 s shout that grants endurance charges to you AND allied players, plus a regen burst and taunt - give it its own key if you take it. A casual pilot can skip it; the drugger keeps charges up regardless.

Common mistakes
  • Attacking in party after the axe upgrade. The purchasable 500%-CDR Uul-Netol's Kiss always keeps 'Curse Enemies with Vulnerability on Hit' - one swing evicts the carry's curse (limit 1, newest wins) and tanks the party's damage. Fix: left-click = move only, Sunder off the bar in party, nothing ever linked to General's Cry.
  • Mashing Q+W+E together. Each plant consumes ALL Valour, so the first banner gets 50 and the other two plant as near-empty duds. Fix: ~3 seconds between banner presses; in short fights just plant Defiance and move on.
  • Planting while running. A banner ends early the moment you leave its bubble - planted mid-sprint it buffs nobody. Fix: plant only where the party is actually fighting; abandoning a banner when the party moves is fine (30% Valour refund).
  • Re-pressing a banner key while that banner is still up. Recasting ends the active banner and consumes your current Valour for a replacement - a net loss mid-fight. Fix: re-press only after it expired, or after the fight has moved somewhere new.
  • Standing at range 'to be safe'. Every single thing this build provides - Fortification share, banner auras, 5%/s regen, taunts - is a proximity effect with roughly a 6-metre reach. At range you are contributing literally zero. Fix: your safe spot IS next to the carry; you are the tankiest character in the party.
  • Expecting warcry buffs. Autoexertion's text removes all warcry buffs and charges for you AND allies - no Rallying damage, no Enduring charges from the 6L. If you want the Enduring Cry regen burst, that is the endgame manual glove cast; party charges come from the drugger either way.

  • Buying utility flasks or 'of the Antidote' mods once the Bloodline plan is in motion. Toad's Price disables utility flasks outright and makes you poison-immune. Fix: stop investing now; end state is 4 life + 1 mana.

How to play

Cheat card

  • STAND STILL AND HOLD SPARK. It fires in a circle from your body, so your feet are the aim, not your cursor. Standing still is also what keeps consecrated ground under you, which is your entire ES sustain via Pious Path.
  • RAMP IS ~10 SECONDS OF UNBROKEN CASTING. Casting gains Intensity; Nadir Mode bleeds it back off every 0.5s; every 6 Intensity lost is one Overload stack (+1% crit, +40% multi), three stacks max. Then you hold there while you keep casting.
  • FROSTBLINK HAS NO COOLDOWN - the price is the 0.85s cast and a full Intensity dump. Free between packs, expensive mid-boss. Sidestep instead when the mechanic allows.
  • YOUR HEALTH BAR IS ENERGY SHIELD. After the swap your life is pinned at 50% forever and that is correct. An ES bar that stops refilling is the emergency, not the life bar.
  • YOU ARE THE ONLY CURSER. Conductivity, on Arcanist Brand, is correct for you and stays correct - you are pure lightning with no Lycia.
Keybinds
  • Spark of the Nova on a KEYBOARD key you can hold, never left mouse. Left click stays Move Only - if Spark is on it you will walk-cancel your own casts, and movement is what drains Intensity and drops your consecrated ground.
  • Frostblink of Wintry Blast near your movement hand. It has no cooldown, so the only thing stopping you spamming it is that each cast is 0.85s of not dealing damage plus an instant Intensity reset.
  • Automation needs one activation and then never again - it is a reservation, so it survives death and zone changes. It triggers Steelskin and Withering Step for you.
  • Conductivity on Arcanist Brand: one key, re-cast per pack. It is your only curse and the party's only curse.
  • Flasks: The Sorrow of the Divine is your panic button (consecrated ground on use, which Pious Path turns into ES recovery) - use it in bad moments rather than hoarding it. Keep the Divine Life Flask and the Eternal Mana Flask of Grounding until the swap; after the swap the mana flask is dead weight because Blood Magic leaves you no mana pool.
  • Bind nothing that curses on hit, and check no corrupted glove implicit does either - 3.29 added curse-on-hit glove corrupts, and any of them would evict your own Conductivity.
Map loop
  1. RUN between packs and let Intensity fall to zero. That is not waste: Pinpoint gives +3 projectiles and removes one per Intensity, so at 0 Intensity you fire the widest nova (10 from a level-20 gem, +2 at 20% quality, +3 from Pinpoint = 15) and at 3 Intensity you fire 12 but each hits 60% harder.
  2. FROSTBLINK INTO the pack rather than beside it - teleporting dumps all Intensity instantly, so your first cast on arrival is guaranteed to be the widest one.
  3. STOP MOVING BEFORE YOU CAST. Standing still is what maintains consecrated ground under you, and after the swap it is also what keeps Arctic Armour's damage reduction active.
  4. HOLD SPARK and let the churn run. You gain Intensity per cast and Nadir Mode strips one every 0.5s, so you hover near maximum while continuously banking the losses that become Overload stacks.
  5. DO NOT AIM. Spark of the Nova launches sparks in all directions that move unpredictably until they hit something or expire after 2 seconds. Stand in the MIDDLE of the pack so the nova radiates through everything; standing at the edge wastes half of it.
  6. PREFER CORRIDORS AND DOORWAYS. Sparks bounce off terrain, so enclosed space recycles strays back into the pack. Open fields waste projectiles.
  7. MOVE AGAIN WITHIN ABOUT 4 SECONDS of stopping. Pious Path makes the consecrated ground you create linger 4 seconds, so a hop-stop-hop rhythm keeps ES recovery running continuously.
  8. Let Automation handle Steelskin - but know it is roughly a 5-second cycle with only about a third uptime. There is a naked window every cycle; defence is not automatic.
  9. WATCH THE ES BAR. After the swap the life bar is pinned at 50% and should never move. If ES stops refilling, break line of sight for about two seconds to restart the recharge and get back onto consecrated ground.
Boss loop
  • Open with Conductivity - it is -36% lightning resistance on a build that is entirely lightning, and it multiplies with both Lightning Penetration and shock.
  • Then stand and cast without interruption. Your damage climbs for roughly the first 10 seconds as Overload reaches 3 stacks; anything that makes you stop resets that climb.
  • DO NOT FROSTBLINK MID-DAMAGE. It dumps every point of Intensity instantly, costing you up to 60% more damage plus the re-ramp. A 0.5-second sidestep only costs a single Intensity - step, do not blink, whenever the mechanic allows it.
  • Keep The Sorrow of the Divine down in the arena and re-press as it fades. Consecrated ground is your ES recovery, and at a boss you cannot rely on hop-stop-hop to maintain it.
  • Stay inside the drugger's warcries and the banner's bubbles. Ancestral Cry's +3% maximum elemental resistance is worth more to you than any single piece of gear you can buy this week.
  • Expect Steelskin to be down about two thirds of the time and plan your positioning around that, not around the buff icon.
Positioning

  • You are the member most likely to outrun the group. Spark clears fast and it is genuinely tempting to keep moving - but every one of your defensive layers except your own gear belongs to somebody else's radius.

Map loop
  1. ZONE-IN STEP 1 (about 8 seconds, every single map, no exceptions): glance at your buff bar and count SEVEN aura icons - Wrath, Clarity, Grace, Determination, Haste, Purity of Elements, Precision. Reservations survive death and zone changes, so they should already be there; a missing one means you ran out of mana somewhere and it silently switched off.
  2. ZONE-IN STEP 2: look for the Holy Relic floating beside you. If it is not there, press R. This is the single most-forgotten step and it is the one that costs the carry 27-40% of his spell lightning damage.
  3. ZONE-IN STEP 3: hit the first monster you see once with Smite. That one hit starts the relic's nova healing loop, lights up the party's +10% attack block, and puts Smite's aura - 42 to 805 added lightning to everyone's hits including the carry's Spark - on the party for 4 seconds. Until you land that first hit, four of your systems are switched off.
  4. THE 4-SECOND BEAT. 'Recently' in PoE means the past 4 seconds, both of your Ascendant block buffs are gated on it, and Smite's own aura lasts exactly 4 seconds. So your entire moment-to-moment loop is a 4-second metronome: ATTACK something real, CAST something, move, repeat. Set the rhythm and everything else takes care of itself.
  5. BEAT ITEM A - ATTACK (every <=4s): land one Smite hit or one Shield Charge on an actual monster. It triggers the Holy Relic's nova (373 Life/s to minions at relic level 14 - the only thing healing the relic against Guardian's Blessing's degen), refreshes '+10% Chance to Block Attack Damage' for you and nearby allies, and refreshes Smite's aura. 3.29 patched out hitting a barrel: it must be a real enemy, and the hit must actually land.
  6. BEAT ITEM B - CAST (every <=4s): press Convocation once it is socketed. Instant, 3.00s cooldown, it refreshes '+10% Chance to Block Spell Damage' for you and nearby allies AND yanks the relic back to your feet so its healing nova lands on itself and on you. Convocation is not on your character yet - socket it first (gloves, unsupported). Until then, use Frostblink to satisfy the cast condition.
  7. BEAT ITEM C - MOVE: Shield Charge to hold 5 metres from the carry. Do not walk. You have 20% movement speed on your boots and Haste on yourself, but use Shield Charge because it doubles as beat item A.
  8. MID-MAP SELF-CHECK, roughly every 30 seconds: seven aura icons, relic visible, carry inside the circle you could cross with one Shield Charge. Three glances, no keypresses. Everything that silently fails on this character fails one of those three checks.
  9. WHAT YOU DO NOT DO IN MAPS: do not throw Stormblast Mines (they are in the stash - see the boss loop for why), do not plant Rejuvenation Totem (its aura radius is 10 units, one metre, so it only works when the party is standing still), and do not chase kills. You have no damage and no business tagging monsters the carry is already deleting.
  10. AFTER EVERY DEATH: your reserved auras are still running - do not re-cast them. What you lost and must restore: the Holy Relic (press R and confirm it is there), Rejuvenation Totem if you had one down, Smite's aura, and [PHRECIA ON] the link. Walk back in, resummon, land a Smite hit, THEN rejoin the fight. Rejoining first and buffing second is how you die twice.
  11. [PHRECIA ON, only if you kept the gloves] Cast Flame Link on the carry the instant you zone in - until it lands, NOBODY in the party has a single aura, including allies standing on top of you - and recast every ~7 seconds, on a rhythm rather than on an alert. Its 9.6s timer is the optimistic case: the link also breaks if the carry leaves your range or line of sight for 4 continuous seconds. Budget 30.83 mana per SECOND per link against Clarity's ~52/s, which funds exactly one link and nothing else.
Boss loop
  • BEFORE THE PULL (10 seconds of prep at the fight-stop): 1) confirm the Holy Relic is alive and next to you, 2) confirm all seven aura icons, 3) plant Rejuvenation Totem at the spot the party will actually stand - its aura radius is 10 units, one metre, and the totem lasts 8 seconds, so you and the relic must be standing ON it, not near it. This is the one place in the game the totem is worth a keypress.
  • OPENING: Smite the boss immediately, then keep landing a hit every <=4 seconds for the whole fight. On a boss there is always something to hit, so this is the easiest part of the fight to get right - and it is the part people forget, because they switch into 'watch the mechanics' mode and stop pressing attack. If you stop attacking on a 3-minute boss, the relic dies about 10 seconds later (16 seconds with the level-1 Guardian's Blessing) and the carry loses Wrath for the rest of the fight.
  • POSITION: stand BEHIND the drugger, on the carry's side of the boss, within 5 metres of the carry. The drugger taunts everything onto himself; that is your cover. You have 2,140 life, 964 ES, no block, and 11,377 armour that will not survive a pinnacle slam - you cannot be the closest body to a telegraph. If the boss turns toward you, Shield Charge or Frostblink out and re-enter behind the drugger.
  • MAINTAIN, in this priority when things get busy: (1) Smite hit, (2) Convocation, (3) [PHRECIA ON] Flame Link recast, (4) everything else. If you only have time for one button, it is Smite - it is the one that carries three party buffs at once.
  • NO VAAL BUTTONS EXIST ON THIS CHARACTER. Vaal Haste and Vaal Discipline are not socketed. Do not go looking for a burst window button you do not have; if you want one, buy and socket the gem first (see build alerts).
  • STORMBLAST MINES - not at all for now. What they actually do is real: each mine applies 3% increased Damage Taken to enemies near it (4% at 20% quality, capped at 150%), it is tagged Aura so your +68% aura effect scales it to about 5% per mine, and Minefield L17 throws 4 additional mines per cast. That is a genuine ~25-40% increased damage taken on the target. The cost is the problem: 0.75s base cast, Minefield's 47% less throwing speed, and 10% more time per additional mine works out to roughly 2 seconds of standing still per throw, for mines that expire in 5 seconds. Holding it up would consume nearly half your time - exactly the seconds you owe Smite and Convocation. Leave them stashed until the core loop is muscle memory, then revisit for pinnacle bosses only.
  • IF YOU DIE: the whole party loses everything at once - all seven auras (you are out of radius), Perquil's Lucky hits, both block buffs, Smite's aura, and the Sign's ailment redirect. Run back, resummon the relic, land a Smite hit, and only then step into the fight. Losing 15 seconds is cheaper than losing the buffs twice.
  • [PHRECIA ON] IF THE CARRY DIES, YOU DIE TOO, INSTANTLY. 'You die if Linked target dies' is live in 3.29 on both Flame Link and Protective Link. There is no counterplay other than not being linked to someone who dies. This is the single strongest argument for taking the gloves off.
  • [PHRECIA ON] Re-link the carry fresh at the fight-stop so you enter the fight with full link duration, and treat the recast as priority 3 for the whole fight. If you kept the gloves, an unlinked ally has zero auras at any distance for as long as the link is down.
Positioning
  • THE REAL LEASH IS 5 METRES, NOT YOUR AURA CIRCLE. Perquil's Toe's 'Nearby Allies' Damage with Hits is Lucky' has a fixed 5 metre radius, and it is an ITEM modifier, not an aura from a skill - so it gets nothing from your +116% increased Area of Effect of Aura Skills, and it never grows. That 5 m is the distance you actually have to hold.
  • TIGHTER STILL: the Ascendant Guardian node's +10% attack block and +10% spell block for 'you and nearby Allies' uses the ascendancy 'nearby' bucket, which GGG has stated is radius 40 - four metres - and is also not scaled by your aura AoE. Treat 4-5 m as your hard leash and everything else takes care of itself.
  • YOUR AURAS ARE GENEROUS BY COMPARISON, AND THAT IS THE TRAP. At +116% Area of Effect of Aura Skills (radius multiplier 1.47): Grace reaches ~7.9 m, Determination and Discipline ~7.8 m, Purity of Elements ~7.5 m. Wrath and Haste have base radius 60 rather than 36 - poedb's numbers put them at ~11.2 m while Path of Building models them at 8.1 m, so expect anywhere in that 8-11 m range and do not be alarmed when PoB shows the lower figure. Either way you can be 8 metres out, still see every aura icon on the carry, and have already lost Lucky hits and both block buffs.
  • SMITE'S AURA is 4.5 m base at the level-30 Smite your sceptre grants, so about 6.6 m for you, and its lightning arcs to one enemy within 5 metres. Use that arc as your in-game ruler: if the carry is further from you than Smite's lightning would jump, you are out of position.
  • THE HOLY RELIC drifts even though Meat Shield makes it Defensive and it is designed to stay near you. Convocation (3.00s cooldown) snaps it to your feet, and this matters because the healing nova radiates from the RELIC - a drifting relic is a relic healing empty floor instead of healing itself.
  • REJUVENATION TOTEM has a 10-unit (one metre) aura radius. You must literally stand on it, and so must the relic. Fight-stop tool, never a mapping tool.
  • WHO MUST BE NEAR WHOM: you near the CARRY (5 m) - he is where buffs convert into damage. You BEHIND the drugger - he taunts, he is your shield. Inside the banner Champion's Fortification sharing range - his Fortitude giving you max Fortification stacks (~1% less damage from all hits per stack, ~20 stacks) is a large slice of your survivability that you do not control. The relic near YOU. Later, the Animate Guardian near the CARRY, because Leer Cast's +50% increased Damage radiates from the AG, not from you.
  • FAILURE MODE WHEN YOU DRIFT IS COMPLETELY SILENT. No warning sound, no icon greying out, no damage number changing colour. The carry's damage just quietly drops, the party's block quietly goes to zero, and everybody blames the map. [PHRECIA ON] the one thing the gloves give back is 'Linked Targets always count as in range of Non-Curse Auras from your Skills' - the linked ally gets every aura across the whole map - but Perquil's 5 m, both block buffs, Smite's aura and the Sign's ailment redirect still need you physically close, and the two unlinked allies still get nothing from any aura at any distance.
